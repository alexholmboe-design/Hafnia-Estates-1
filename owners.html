How rent levels work

What Copenhagen apartments actually rent for

Rent in Copenhagen is usually discussed per square metre per month. The city average sits at roughly DKK 210 per m² per month, but the spread between neighbourhoods is wide.

Location moves it most

Indre By, Nordhavn and Frederiksberg sit well above the average. Valby, Brønshøj and the western suburbs sit below it. Between the top and the bottom of the city there is more than 60 percent.

Small homes earn more per m²

A 35 m² studio usually achieves a higher rate per square metre than a 110 m² apartment on the same street. The calculator accounts for that.

Furnished adds about a fifth

Furnished homes let to international tenants generally achieve 15 to 20 percent more than the same home unfurnished, and they let faster.

The rules can cap it

Danish rent regulation can limit what may lawfully be charged, depending on the age of the building and whether it has been improved. The market rate and the lawful rate are not always the same number.
