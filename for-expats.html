Good to know

How the Danish rental market works

Deposit

Landlords typically require a deposit of up to three months' rent plus prepaid rent when the lease is signed.

CPR registration

Your address must be registered with the National Registration Office before you get your CPR number and yellow health card.

The lease

Always make sure it follows the official Danish lease template (A9). It protects both parties, and we insist on it.

Move-in inspection

Legally required since 2015. Done and documented correctly at both move-in and move-out, so deposits come back.
