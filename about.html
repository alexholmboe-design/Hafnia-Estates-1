About us

One city, done properly

Hafnia Homes finds furnished homes for people moving to Copenhagen, and helps property owners rent out to them.

Hafnia is the old Latin name for Copenhagen.

One city, one kind of client. That is a deliberate choice. It means we know which buildings let you register an address, which leases hold up, and what a flat on a given street is actually worth.

Background

Built on experience in the Copenhagen rental market

Hafnia Homes came out of several years of hands-on work with rental housing in Copenhagen. That work made one thing clear. The difficult part of moving here is rarely the job or the visa. It is the apartment, and the gap between what is advertised and what is actually usable to someone arriving from another country.

So that is what the company does, and nothing else.

How we work

Three things we hold to

One

Clear about money

Prices agreed before we start, no fees that appear later, and we never hold anyone's money.

Two

Correct from the start

Danish tenancy law followed properly. Shortcuts in a lease only ever surface later, and always badly.

Three

Both sides treated the same

A tenancy works when both sides feel it was set up fairly. We are not interested in the other kind.
