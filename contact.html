Contact

Tell us what you need

Write to us below, or call during office hours. We reply within 48 hours on weekdays.

Who is writing?

Three kinds of enquiry

Pick the one that fits and the form below will be set up for it.

I am moving to Copenhagen

You need somewhere to live. Tell us your arrival date, your budget and the areas you are considering. There is no fee for tenants.

We are relocating employees

You are bringing people to Copenhagen. Tell us roughly how many and when, and whether they are coming with family.

I have an apartment to rent out

Tell us the address, the size and when it is available. We will come and see it and give you a realistic figure.
What happens next

A person reads it, not a system

Every enquiry is answered by the person who would handle your case. If we are not the right fit, we will say so and point you somewhere better rather than string it out.
