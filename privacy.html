Privacy

How we handle your information

The short version: we collect only what we need to find you a home or a tenant, we sell nothing to anyone, and we delete what we no longer need.

Who we are

Hafnia Homes, Copenhagen, Denmark. Contact us at hello@hafniahomes.dk or +45 22 48 76 97. We are the data controller for the information described here.

If you contact us

When you write to us or use the rent calculator, we receive your name, your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us about your situation or your property. We use it to answer you, and for nothing else. If it does not lead to an agreement, we delete it within twelve months.

If you are looking for a home

To put you forward to an owner, we verify your employment, income, residence permit and identity. We check the documents, record the result, and do not keep copies of your passport or residence permit afterwards. The owner is told what they need in order to decide, and no more than that.

Documents gathered for screening are deleted once a lease is signed, or within three months if you are not selected.

If you let a property through us

We hold your contact details, the details of the property, and the agreement between us. Accounting records are kept for five years, because Danish bookkeeping law requires it.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Write to us and we will reply within a month. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to Datatilsynet, the Danish Data Protection Agency.
