Housing is usually the part of a relocation that goes wrong.

It is also furthest from what an HR team is set up to handle. We take it on entirely, and report back when it is done.

What they arrive to

A home, not a hotel room

Employees on a posting need somewhere they can actually live: a kitchen they will use, a place to work, and an address they can register at. We look for that, rather than for whatever is quickest to book.

What we handle

The housing side, start to finish

Finding the home

Furnished homes matched to what the employee needs, checked as available before you see them.

The lease

Prepared on the official Typeformular A10 with the right clauses for a fixed-term, furnished tenancy.

Ready for arrival

Keys, access and house information in place before the employee lands.

Move-in inspection

Condition and meter readings documented, so there is no argument about the deposit later.

One point of contact

One invoice

Working with HR

One brief, and we take it from there

You send the arrival date, the budget and anything specific about the employee. We come back with homes that fit, and we keep you posted at each step without needing to be chased.

How a placement runs

Five steps

01

You send the brief

Arrival date, budget, whether they are coming with family, and anything specific.

02

We send a shortlist

Two to four suitable homes with photographs and the practical details.

03

We prepare the lease

Terms negotiated and the contract drafted. Deposit and dates confirmed in writing.

04

The employee arrives

Keys and house information ready. Settlement work starts in the first week if booked.

05

We report back

You get confirmation that it is done, and one invoice on the agreed terms.
